# Sample library: sources, licences, credits

Generated by `experiments/make_demo_samples.py` with `samples.json`; edit the script, not this file.

Every sample is a held-out row that AnuLM-Omni-400M did not train on. Media were converted (audio to 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, photos resized to at most 640 px and re-saved as JPEG); the words, transcripts and labels are unchanged. Flickr30k and COCO are deliberately absent: their terms do not allow commercial use.
