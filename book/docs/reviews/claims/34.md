### Findings worth review

- **Porting trap.** The lab book's `answer_logprobs` (course/build/solutions/ch11.py, at the pin) calls the model without targets. On the course's own model that returns every position; on `model.py`'s AnuLM it returns (B, 1, V) and the `gather` raises (shown by the example). The course's Code tour suggests adding a `dpo.py` built on `answer_logprobs`; it would need targets passed (or hidden states) on `model.py`.
- The e^d − d − 1 estimator's two properties are proved in the text from e^d ≥ 1 + d and E_θ[π_ref/π_θ] = 1, and checked numerically; the chapter does not attribute it to a paper. No register row supports "GRPO uses this estimator", so the chapter does not claim it.
- The lab book's Chapter 19 (GRPO) and its `ch19.py` solution are not at the pin (added in d3f1d97); they are cross-linked only. The lab book's Chapter 11 DPO finding (both answers falling) is mentioned qualitatively with no numbers.
- No register proposals.
