# Appendix E  Bibliography

Every external source the book may cite, as `RESEARCH_REFERENCES.md` registers
it. A chapter cites a source by its ID, such as [R12], and only for the claim
in that source's register row; the register also records how each one was
checked and every correction made to it. The main entries (R) are the papers
and documents the book's arguments rest on; the supporting entries (S) give
history and context. Each chapter's own "References" table lists the IDs it
cites and what for.

The first author is the one the register verified; arXiv entries give the year
of the first version. An arXiv identifier `arXiv:NNNN.NNNNN` resolves at
`https://arxiv.org/abs/NNNN.NNNNN`.
