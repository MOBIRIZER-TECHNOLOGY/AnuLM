## Review fixes (2026-11-01)

- E1: 19.1 is rewritten around absorption. The decoupled key exists so that
  W_UK can be folded into the query, which a position-dependent rotation would
  block. Without absorption, c could be cached position-free and rotated after
  expansion. This repository does not absorb, so its split buys no cache saving
  (it caches D_r extra). The comment at `model.py` 515–516 is quoted as saying
  otherwise, and not relied on. The reason is stated without a register source
  (proposal 2 extended).
- E2: 19.5 says absorbing the key half removes only T·D_c·H·D_n (3,538,944 of
  7,077,888 at T = 256); the value half has to go into `o_proj`. Both are
  NOT IN THE REPO and uncited.
- E3: the intro now qualifies the saving ("far smaller than every head keeping
  its own K and V; against GQA it need not be smaller"). A Sarvam 30B row
  (GQA 512; full 8,192) is added to the table, the example and Figure 19.2,
  with the explicit 512 vs 576 comparison.
- T = 1,024 became T = `block_size` = 256. The nano_30b full-K/V cell is filled
  (768). The 576 coincidence is noted.
- A rotated-latent mutant fails the shift test (1.202 on a scale of 1.091)
  while the module passes (1.8e-07); it is quoted in 19.6.
