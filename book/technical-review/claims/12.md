### Repository facts checked by hand

- `generate` never calls the model with S > 1 and `start > 0` (lines 1082–1109):
  the prompt is prefilled at `start = 0`, then `pending = nxt` is one token. The
  rebuild at `block_size` also restarts at 0. `decode_from_embeds` likewise
  (978, 999).
- `GQAttention.forward` appends to the cache and never trims it (441–447), so
  the window does not shrink the cache; shown on `tiny()` by the example.
- `test_model.T` mutants work because `GQAttention.forward` and
  `MLAttention.forward` look up `_attn_mask` as a module global at call time.
