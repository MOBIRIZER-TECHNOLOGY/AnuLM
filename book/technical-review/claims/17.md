### Could not verify / open

- Why `routed_scaling_factor` is 2.5: no reason in the repository; the chapter
  says it follows Sarvam's configuration and shows it is foldable into
  `down_proj`.
- The bf16-autocast measurement emulates the pre-fix gate by calling `_gate`
  under autocast with fp32 inputs; in real bf16 training the inputs to the gate
  would themselves carry bf16 rounding, so the true pre-fix rate on this
  checkpoint could differ. The chapter states the method.
