### Interpretation flagged in the text

- That rare input rows shrank because of weight decay is stated as the likely
  cause, not measured: the run's `--weight-decay` is not stored in the
  checkpoint; the model card names AdamW; `make_optimizer` decays embeddings.
- The ln V + σ²/2 approximation assumes independent Gaussian logits; the
  chapter gives both the prediction and the measured value.

### Review fixes (independent review, 2026-11-01)

All items were re-checked. Two numbers differ slightly from the reviewer's; see items 2 and 4.

1. **Weight decay "not stored in the checkpoint"** (error). Confirmed and corrected:
   - `_v2_run.cmd` and `experiments/v2_train.sh` (tracked) pass no `--weight-decay`, so the default 0.1 applies.
   - `ckpt_base_v2.pt.last` stores weight_decay 0.1 on 1,432 tensors and 0.0 on 81, both groups fused.
   - `ch10_measure_tables.py` now prints those groups and computes decay alone over the run's schedule, using `train.lr_at` with the run's flags: a factor of 10^-45.8, taking 0.640 to 9.0e-47.

   The chapter now says that even the 0.013 rows got occasional gradients, so their size is a balance of gradient against decay, and keeps "nothing here isolates decay as the cause". The 10.7 table row no longer states the cause as fact.
2. **"The two tables are unrelated"** (error). Now "not aligned": a token's two rows point in unrelated directions, with cosine 0.000. Their neighbourhoods do overlap:
   - over 2,000 tokens seen at least 1,000 times, 4.9% of the top-10 neighbours agree between the two tables, as the reviewer found;
   - chance level here is 10/32,767 = 0.031%, where the reviewer had 0.066%, presumably from a different denominator. I kept mine and the text says how it is computed.
3. **The badge cross-reference** (error). Now `(F.embedding, 10.4)`.
4. **The released-size first loss.** The head-only simulation is now labelled *illustrative* and said to run on synthetic inputs. The real released configuration at random initialisation is now quoted with its code: loss 10.621, logit std 0.640 (seed 0, 4 × 256 random ids), 0.224 above ln V. The reviewer got the same loss and a logit std of 0.638.
5. **Exercise 1.** Now asks about `F.one_hot(...).float()`, and notes that `F.one_hot` returns int64.
6. **Pointers to Chapter 7 (7.2).** Added where AdamW's decoupled weight decay appears.
7. **Output-row norms.** The Fig 10.2 caption now says they stay between 0.36 and 0.51 and fall as tokens get more common; the 100k+ bucket keeps 57% (0.365 / 0.640).
8. **The docstring's "~0.4 GFLOP".** Added a note: 393,657 × 1,024 is 0.40 G multiply-adds, which is 0.81 GFLOP per table per forward pass.
9. **`ckpt_base_v2.pt` labelled as if at the pin.** It is now listed as a local file, not in the commit, together with `ckpt_base_v2.pt.last`, `_v2_run.cmd` and `val.u16`.
