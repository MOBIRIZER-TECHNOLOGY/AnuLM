### Findings worth review

- `api.py` discards earlier turns and system messages silently (single-turn); documented in its model description but not in an error.
- The streaming example uses a stand-in engine (byte-level codec from `serve.Engine`, a `tiny()` model) because `Generation.__init__` asks the server's model cache for the chat checkpoint. `Generation.run`, `_release`, `GPU` and `generate` themselves are unchanged repository code.
- The timings printed in step 4 are illustrative of ordering only.
