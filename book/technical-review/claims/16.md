## Review fixes (2026-11-01)

- E1: SiLU is not bounded by 1. 16.3 and the key point now say a gate scales
  the value by any factor from −0.28 upward, about g for large g (SiLU(4) = 3.93).
- E2/E3: 16.7 split. A forgotten transpose (0.5985 on a scale of 0.4609) is
  caught by testing with D ≠ I. A gate/up swap (0.1585, about a third of the
  scale) is not, since both matrices are (I, D); only an output comparison
  catches it.
- E4: the uncited "original FFN, two matrices with a ReLU, 4× wide" sentence is
  removed. The GELU sentence is trimmed to "the GELU is one such activation
  function [S04]". The R01 proposal is withdrawn.
- 16.6: "Few gates shut" became "most gates damp, almost none shut" (58.6%
  negative, so at most 0.28× and sign-flipped; none below −4). "Most-used
  channel" is defined (channel 1,203, largest total Σh², 1.5%). 12.08 vs 0.73
  are now stated as L2 norms (32× RMS: 0.378 and 0.023), with a cross-reference
  to Ch 15. The layer-10 ratio is flagged as including position 0. The hook
  excerpt shows the per-channel lines, with the elision marked `# ...`.
- `transformers_wrapper_matches_the_native_model` is flagged: it returns early,
  and counts as a pass, without `transformers` (test lines 1281–1286).
- Optional item done: 2,816 = 2,730.67 rounded up to a multiple of 256, printed
  by `ch16_swiglu.py`, with "the code does not say that this is how it was
  chosen".
- MoE motivation softened ("Chapter 17 builds that idea into the layer's
  structure").
