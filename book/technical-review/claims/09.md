### Repository facts checked by hand

- `release/AnuLM-Base-v2-400M/tokenizer.multi32k.json` and
  `data/multi32k.json` have identical merge lists (md5 of the JSON-dumped
  merges, 2b819a86…).
- `tokenizer.json`'s pre-tokenizer is `Split` on `\s*\S+|\s+` then
  `ByteLevel` without its own regex; EOS 32,767 as `<|endoftext|>`.
- `experiments/build_v2.py`'s docstring names
  `release/AnuLM-Base-400M/tokenizer.json`, not the v2 folder; the chapter
  quotes only that the docstring says the Rust build was checked identical on
  400 + 400 documents, and the book's own check uses the v2 folder's file.
- The tie-breaking claim (smaller pair wins on equal counts) follows from
  `heapq` ordering of `(-count, pair)` tuples and is exercised by the toy
  (`(l, o)` vs `(o, w)`, both 8).
