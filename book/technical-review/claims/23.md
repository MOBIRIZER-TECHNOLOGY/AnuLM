### Open questions and things not verified

- **Step times are not used.** `ch23_measure.py` also records ms/step, but another writer's job
  shared the GPU during one of my two runs (nvidia-smi showed a second python process at 57%), and
  the five timed steps varied by up to 2×. The chapter quotes no timing of its own; the
  checkpointing cost is RESULTS §32's +30%. The JSON keeps the last run's times (plain 324 ms,
  checkpointing 371 ms, chunked 297 ms, both 445 ms; each the median of five steps) for a reviewer
  who reruns on an idle card. Memory peaks were identical in both runs.
- The `warn_if_spilling` caveat (step 0 reserves 13.62 GiB, later steps 15.12 GiB) is measured on
  random weights; the released run's own log shows no WARNING line and its median 325 ms/step
  matches the plain configuration here, so the card did not spill in practice.
- `docs/RESULTS.md` §4 does not name the hardware for its ms/step figures; the chapter says
  "in eager mode" only.

## Review fixes (2026-11-01)

- **E1 router row.** Dropped the misleading "router weights" row. `ch23_precision.py` now wraps
  one router's `_gate` and records the dtype of its `F.linear` output: float32 through

  behaviour). Code (`spy_on_gate`) and output quoted in 23.2.

  23.4 and the key point use it. `ch23_measure.py --report` prints the step-0 peak 9.27 GiB (no
  moments yet) against 12.26 GiB later: +2.99 GiB against moments of 2.96 GiB. The figure asserts
  4.44 and 2.99.
