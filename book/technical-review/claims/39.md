### Findings worth review

- The exporter's comment (271–274) and the wrapper's warning say 16-bit router biases "collapse" the output. Rounding only the biases (everything else fp32) cost +0.003 nats on 32,732 tokens: a measurable loss, not a collapse. Full 16-bit loading was not measured. The chapter states both.
- The native fp32 loss here (3.37908) differs from Chapter 20's 3.37895 on the same windows by 1.3e-4; Chapter 20's script ran a different MoE path/device settings. Not investigated.
- The released folder's `model.py` is a snapshot that differs from the pin; whether the Hub copy equals the local copy was not checked (no download).
