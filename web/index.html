The model continues whatever you type.

This is a base model, not a chatbot: it was trained to continue Hindi text from Wikipedia and Wikisource, so it answers the way an article or a story would carry on from your words. It is fluent and it invents facts — every date, number and name is fiction. A title followed by a blank line (the training document format) gives the most article-like output; a phrase like उसने कहा gives narrative.
